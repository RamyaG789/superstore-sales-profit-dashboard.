# Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

The **Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence solution designed to transform transactional sales data into meaningful business insights. It provides a clear overview of sales performance, profitability, products, customers, categories, and regional trends while supporting data-driven decision-making.

## Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard tracks essential KPIs, including:

* **Total Sales** – Overall revenue generated.
* **Total Profit** – Total profit from transactions.
* **Total Quantity** – Number of products sold.
* **Profit Margin** – Profit relative to sales.
* **Total Orders** – Number of unique orders.
* **Average Order Value** – Average revenue per order.

## Dashboard Analysis

Users can analyze:

* Sales and profit trends over time
* Regional and state-level performance
* Category and sub-category performance
* Customer segment contribution
* Product-level sales and profitability
* Discount and profit relationships
* Order and shipping performance
* Top-performing and underperforming products
* Geographic distribution of sales and profit

Interactive filters allow users to explore data by **Order Date, Region, State, Category, Sub-Category, Segment, Ship Mode, and Product**.

## Dataset

The dataset contains transactional information including **Row ID, Order ID, Order Date, Ship Date, Ship Mode, Customer ID, Customer Name, Segment, Country/Region, City, State, Postal Code, Region, Product ID, Category, Sub-Category, Sales, Quantity, Discount, and Profit**.

## Business Objective

The primary objective is to convert raw sales data into an interactive analytical solution that helps users understand **what drives sales, what generates profit, and where improvement opportunities exist**.

The dashboard can identify high-performing products and categories, profitable and underperforming regions, valuable customer segments, sales trends, discount-related profitability issues, and areas with high sales but relatively low profit.

## Dashboard Highlights

The dashboard combines **KPI cards, time-series charts, bar charts, category analysis, regional analysis, product performance, customer segmentation, geographic visualizations, and interactive filters** to provide a comprehensive view of business performance.

This project demonstrates how **data analytics, visualization, business intelligence, and AI-assisted development** can transform structured transactional data into an intuitive dashboard that communicates business performance and supports informed decision-making.
